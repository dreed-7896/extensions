R2 Library JSON

One details.json with a chapters array. Drop it in the series folder. Split files still work.

Series

Cover: Chapter 1_1 = first page of the chapter titled Chapter 1. Or a direct image URL.

Chapters

Sources in list order. A gallery URL is one chapter. A NovelCrow / AllPornComic / MangaDex series URL expands to every chapter on that title. Blank Ch. = all, including chapters that show up later. Replacing details.json in the bucket is the whole update.

Ch. (series only): 2- = from 2 through newest, 2-10 = those chapters, 5 = only 5. Name fields use that source’s chapter numbers. Pages crops images: 50 / 3-50 / 3-.

Row order is the chapter order. Names on a series are optional.
